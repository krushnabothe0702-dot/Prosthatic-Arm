Robotic Hand

A photo-based project page documenting a 3D-printed hand prototype, its visible parts, and the current build.

Project overview

This prototype has a printed hand-shaped body with separate finger sections and a wider cuff or forearm attachment. Thin cords are routed through and around the fingers and palm. The photos capture the physical build from two angles.

Build3D-printed white plastic parts
Visible mechanismArticulated finger sections with routed cords

What is visible

  • A palm section with a repeated pattern of small openings.
  • Separate printed segments form the fingers and thumb.
  • Thin cords pass along the fingers and connect toward the hand and cuff.
  • A broad printed cuff-like piece extends from the wrist area.

These notes describe only what can be seen in the photos. The images do not show the control system, materials beyond the printed parts and cords, or whether the hand has been tested.
